About Me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Mathematics in the Joint Graduate Program (UV-PUCV-USM) in Chile, supervised by Prof. Rodrigo Lecaros (UTFSM) and co-supervised by Prof. Ariel Pérez (UBB). My work lies at the intersection of control theory, inverse problems, and stochastic partial differential equations (SPDEs).

My research focuses on the development of Carleman estimates adapted to semi-discrete stochastic operators in arbitrary dimensions, with applications to observability inequalities, null controllability, and inverse random source and Cauchy problems.

Research Interests: Inverse Problems, Controllability, Carleman Estimates, and Stochastic Partial Differential Equations (SPDEs).
