About Me

Publications & Preprints Grants & Projects Teaching Talks & Events Academic Service

I am a PhD Candidate in Mathematics in the joint doctoral program between UTFSM, UV, and PUCV. My research focuses on control theory, inverse problems, and stochastic partial differential equations (SPDEs).
